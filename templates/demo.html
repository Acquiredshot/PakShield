Why enterprises buy this

Agentic data exfiltration is the real enterprise risk

Encryption alone does not stop a bad vendor prompt, a compromised toolchain, or a multi-agent escalation path. The platform prevents leakage, denies unauthorized execution, and preserves firm evidence for every decision.

1. DLP + anonymization

PII, API keys, and internal identifiers are tokenized before vendor data ever reaches the agent context or model memory.

2. Proof of execution

Each vendor submission, rule decision, and agent action is data-hashed and signed to create cryptographic evidence for audits and disputes.

3. Guardrails + HITL

Agents are restricted by vendor scope, token expiry, and escalating approval gates tied to real-world business risk.

Dynamic blast-radius governance

Risk-scored actions trigger the right control

Every action is scored by transaction value, vendor trust, data sensitivity, and anomaly indicators. As the blast radius grows, the workflow automatically moves from autonomous execution to multi-agent consensus to human signoff.
